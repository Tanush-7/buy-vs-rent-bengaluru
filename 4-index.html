What the model counts, and what it doesn't

Read this before acting on the verdict.

Counted

EMI split into interest and principal, maintenance and tanker water on the buy side, stamp duty and interiors upfront, rent growing yearly on the rent side, and both sides' spare cash compounding at your investment return.

Not counted

Tax breaks (old-regime home-loan interest and HRA — you can't claim both fully), selling costs and capital-gains tax at exit, society sinking-fund shocks, and the fact that ownership caps your mobility.

The real drivers

At Bengaluru's 2026 prices, rent is usually 2.5–3.5% of the flat's value per year while a loan costs ~8%. Buying wins in the model when appreciation stays high or you hold very long; renting plus investing wins when it doesn't. These are assumptions to stress-test, not predictions — the knobs above show exactly where the answer flips.
